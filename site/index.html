Alexa, after the Echo

Alexa was an ordinary girl's name for a generation before the Echo. Births climbed from the late 1980s to about 6,000 a year. Amazon delivered the first Echos to invited customers at the end of 2014. Births held up in 2015, when most parents had still never met the device, and have fallen every year since.
